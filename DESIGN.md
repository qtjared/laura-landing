# Laura Flores Hairstylist Design System

## Direction

The visual world is a chromatic consultation folio: the precision of professional hair-color swatches combined with the generous scale and photography of a fashion lookbook. It avoids the category's predictable beige salon template and decorative beauty-script styling.

## Color

- `porcelain`: cool near-white page ground.
- `mist`: pale blue-gray secondary surface.
- `graphite`: softened black for primary text and the closing section.
- `burgundy`: muted wine accent reserved for actions and editorial markers.
- `taupe`: cool brown-gray supporting tone.
- Burgundy is the only action color. Body copy always uses graphite or a high-contrast tint of its section foreground.

## Typography

- Bodoni Moda is reserved for the Laura Flores wordmark and short display phrases. Its fashion heritage supports the salon context without becoming ornamental script.
- Manrope carries navigation, body copy, labels, and buttons.
- Headlines stay concise, with tight but unclipped line-height. Body copy stays within 65 characters per line.

## Composition

- The desktop hero is an asymmetric editorial split, not a centered marketing stack.
- Photography is cropped decisively and presented in varied ratios like a contact sheet.
- Thin rules and open space group information; cards and heavy shadows are avoided.
- Corners are nearly square. Buttons alone use a full pill to make actions unmistakable.
- Mobile layouts collapse to one clear column with horizontal gallery overflow where it preserves scale.
- The studio begins a deliberate dark closing chapter. Its overlapping photographs feel spatial, while Drinks uses a simpler image-and-copy split for a quieter pause.

## Interaction

- Links and buttons use visible focus rings, clear labels, and small physical press feedback.
- The only authored motion is the first-view reveal of the image and chromatic bands, implemented in CSS and disabled for reduced-motion users.
- Photography never zooms or moves on hover.

## Imagery

- Images should emphasize healthy texture, dimensional color, and the quiet confidence of the styling process.
- Temporary stock imagery is explicitly disclosed and never captioned as Laura's client work.
- Real images should eventually replace files in `public/images` while keeping the existing aspect ratios.
- Temporary studio and coffee images carry the same explicit reference disclosure as the gallery.
