# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Women in Villahermosa, Tabasco who are evaluating a local hairstylist and want expert guidance before choosing a hair color or care service.

## Product Purpose

Present Laura Flores Hairstylist's specialty in colorimetry and hair care, help prospective clients understand her approach, and move appointment inquiries to WhatsApp.

## Positioning

Laura presents hair color as a personalized decision informed by colorimetry and the condition of each client's hair, rather than as a one-size-fits-all service.

## Operating Context

Prospective clients often discover Laura through Instagram, review visual examples of hair color work, and contact her on WhatsApp to discuss services and appointments.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The site is Spanish-first.
- The business is based in Villahermosa, Tabasco.
- Appointments and service inquiries are managed through WhatsApp at `+52 993 418 9811`.
- Prices are not published on the website.
- Coffee is available to enjoy in the studio or take away.
- The initial site is a single landing page built with Next.js and React.
- The site must remain responsive across screen sizes and aspect ratios.

## Brand Commitments

- Business name: Laura Flores Hairstylist.
- Specialty: Colorimetry and hair care.
- Instagram: `https://www.instagram.com/laurafloreshairstylist/`.
- The experience should feel elegant and friendly, using cool, low-saturation white, gray, muted burgundy, brown, and off-black.
- Use a custom typographic wordmark until a formal logo is supplied.

## Evidence on Hand

- Laura's public Instagram profile is the current visual reference.
- Real portfolio photography will be supplied later by the site owner.
- Real photography of the studio will be supplied later by the site owner.
- Stock photography may be used temporarily and must not be presented as Laura's own work.
- No testimonials, service prices, or performance claims are available and none may be fabricated.

## Product Principles

- Make Laura's specialty and location clear within the first viewport.
- Keep every appointment path direct, clearly labeled, and connected to WhatsApp.
- Let photography demonstrate aesthetic taste without misrepresenting temporary stock images as client results.
- Favor calm expertise over sales pressure or beauty-industry hype.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain readable contrast, visible keyboard focus, clear action labels, semantic structure, useful alternative text, and reduced-motion support.
